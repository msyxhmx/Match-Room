# Demo script (about 3 minutes) — draft

1. **Problem (20s)**: half-time is 15 minutes where fans and studios want to know what happened and what's next.
2. **Replay to 45' (30s)**: pick a match, replay the first half, goals and cards tick by.
3. **Half-time page (60s)**: the story cards and their evidence; the result bar; next-goal odds.
4. **Why it's explainable (40s)**: top factors (SHAP), what to watch, every claim linked to events.
5. **How it's built (20s)**: five-stage pipeline, synthetic data, Azure.
6. **Close (10s)**: model beats the score-only baseline by <metric>.
