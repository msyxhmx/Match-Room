# Club identities (crest round 3)

Fictional clubs, fictional towns. These sheets give each crest a local story to come from, the
way a real club's crest does (an arms factory, an ironworks, a hat trade, a landmark). They are
design notes only: team names and data in `sim/` don't change.

**Colours.** Each club's primary comes from `web/src/clubColours.ts`. Northgate Blue `#1D4ED8`
and Vale Claret `#8B1E3F` are locked. Accents come from one shared list, so the eight still read
as one league:
- white `#FFFFFF`
- crest gold `#C9A13B` (accent only, always on the club colour, never as a crest's outer edge)
- ink `#1D1D1F`

**Lettering.** Oswald (SIL OFL 1.1, https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oswald), a
condensed sans, converted to outlines. The font file isn't committed.

**Banned emblems** (too close to real clubs): lion, cannon, cockerel, Liver bird, hammers, fox,
seagull, canary, wolf, ram, bee, eagle, ship, a tree as the main emblem, seahorse, crossed blades,
black-and-white stripes, a halo or saint.

## NGC · Northgate City
- **Ground:** Northgate Stadium. **Founded:** 1879. **Nickname:** the Gatemen.
- **Story:** The old walled town had four gates. Only the north gate survived the 1860s
  clearances, and the club was founded by clerks from the toll house beside it. Fans still walk
  through the arch on match days.
- **Motto:** *Porta Patens*, "the gate stands open".
- **Colours:** Northgate Blue, white, crest-gold accent.
- **Emblem:** the gatehouse, with the north star above it. The gate is the town's one landmark,
  and the star points the way north.

## KST · Kingston Vale
- **Ground:** Vale Road. **Founded:** 1894. **Nickname:** the Herons.
- **Story:** A market town in a river valley. Herons fished the shallows below the town bridge,
  and the founders played on the water meadow beside them. The meadow flooded every spring, so
  the club moved up to Vale Road.
- **Motto:** *Patience and Purpose*.
- **Colours:** Vale Claret, white, crest-gold accent.
- **Emblem:** a heron standing in the river, between the valley's two slopes. The heron is the
  patient fisher; the slopes are the vale.

## RIV · Riverside Rovers
- **Ground:** Riverside Park. **Founded:** 1886. **Nickname:** the Kingfishers.
- **Story:** Founded by ferrymen and lock-keepers who "roved" the river between towns for away
  games. The kingfisher was the boatmen's lucky sign: a flash of colour meant fair water.
- **Motto:** *Swift and True*.
- **Colours:** River Teal, white, crest-gold accent.
- **Emblem:** a diving kingfisher above a river line. It's fast and bright, and it doesn't need a
  frame.

## HAR · Harbour Athletic
- **Ground:** The Quayside. **Founded:** 1883. **Nickname:** the Keepers.
- **Story:** A fishing harbour whose lighthouse keepers and dockers formed an athletic club for
  running, rowing and football. Football outlived the rest. The ground sits on the old quay.
- **Motto:** *Lux in Tempestate*, "light in the storm".
- **Colours:** Harbour Gold, ink, white.
- **Emblem:** the harbour lighthouse with an anchor. The light guided boats home, and the anchor
  stands for the dockers.

## ELM · Elmstead United
- **Ground:** Elmstead Lane. **Founded:** 1902. **Nickname:** the Leaves.
- **Story:** Two village sides, Elmstead and Elmstead Green, merged in 1902 ("United"). The lane
  between them was lined with elms that were lost to disease in the 1970s. The club kept the leaf.
- **Motto:** *Two Fields, One Club*.
- **Colours:** Elm Green, white, crest-gold accent.
- **Emblem:** a single elm leaf, split in two colours for the two villages that joined. It's a
  leaf, not a tree.

## MIL · Millbrook Town
- **Ground:** Millbrook Meadow. **Founded:** 1888. **Nickname:** the Millers.
- **Story:** A mill town on a brook. The waterwheel at Corn Mill Lane turned for 300 years, and
  the club began as the mill workers' Saturday team on the meadow behind it.
- **Motto:** *Turn and Turn Again*.
- **Colours:** Mill Black, white, crest-gold accent.
- **Emblem:** the waterwheel in the brook, with the name set above it like the mill's painted
  sign.

## ASH · Ashford Albion
- **Ground:** Albion Ground. **Founded:** 1890. **Nickname:** the Cliffs.
- **Story:** A coastal town under white chalk cliffs ("Albion" is the old name for Britain, after
  those cliffs), where a ford crossed the stream by an ash grove. The crest is quartered from the
  old town arms.
- **Motto:** *Albion Stands*.
- **Colours:** Albion Magenta, white, ink.
- **Emblem:** four quarters: the ash sprig, the white cliffs, the ford's stepping stones and the
  year.

## QNB · Queensbridge FC
- **Ground:** Bridge End. **Founded:** 1897. **Nickname:** the Bridgemen.
- **Story:** The town grew round a stone bridge opened by a queen in 1630, hence the name and the
  crown. The ground is at the far end of the bridge.
- **Motto:** *Over and Onward*.
- **Colours:** Bridge Olive, white, crest-gold accent.
- **Emblem:** the three-arched bridge under a simple crown. The bridge is the town; the crown is
  its name.
